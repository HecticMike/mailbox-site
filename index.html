MailboxOne

MailboxOne is a desktop email and calendar client for Windows, built for Gmail and Google Calendar. Folders, conversations, a reading pane, fast search, and a shortcut on every command. Your mail stays on your computer.

  • Several Gmail accounts, each with its own name and colour
  • Essentials and Extras: your inbox split into people and updates versus promotions and social
  • Clean up: see who fills your inbox and unsubscribe safely
  • New-mail pop-ups, a tray flag, calendar with invites and repeating events
  • Private by design: no servers, no tracking, no ads
