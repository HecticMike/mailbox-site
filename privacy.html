MailboxOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

MailboxOne is a desktop application that runs on your own computer and connects directly to your Google account(s) to show and manage your email and calendar. The developer operates no servers for MailboxOne and receives none of your data.

What MailboxOne accesses

With your permission, MailboxOne uses Google's Gmail and Google Calendar APIs to read, send and organise your email, and to read and edit your calendar events. It asks for these permissions (Gmail: read, compose, send and manage; Calendar: see and edit events) because each feature needs them, and for nothing else. You can use more than one Google account; each is kept separate on your computer.

Where your data goes

Your email and calendar data travels only between Google and your computer. A copy of your messages and events is kept in a local cache on your computer, in a separate folder for each account, so the app is fast and works offline. MailboxOne does not send your data to the developer or to any third party, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

Sign-in credentials

MailboxOne never sees your Google password. Access tokens are stored on your computer in Windows Credential Manager. You can revoke MailboxOne's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by removing the account in the app.

Updates

MailboxOne can check for new versions. To do so it contacts the public releases page of the project on GitHub, which therefore sees your internet address, like any web request. Nothing about you or your mail is sent. Updates are verified with a cryptographic signature before they are installed. You can turn the check off in Settings.

Support reports

"Copy diagnostics" puts the version, your Windows version and the state of your accounts on your clipboard. It never contains your mail, subjects, addresses or names, and is only sent if you choose to paste it into a message to the developer.

What MailboxOne does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • It does not allow humans to read your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MailboxOne's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Removing an account in MailboxOne signs it out, revokes its access with Google and deletes its local cache. Uninstalling MailboxOne offers to delete all local data as well.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/HecticMike/mailbox-site/issues.
