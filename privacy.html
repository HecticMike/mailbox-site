Mailbox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Mailbox is a desktop application that runs on your own computer and connects directly to your Google account to show and manage your email and calendar. The developer operates no servers for Mailbox.

What Mailbox accesses

With your permission, Mailbox uses Google's Gmail and Google Calendar APIs to read, send and organise your email, and to read and edit your calendar events. It only requests the access needed for those features.

Where your data goes

Your email and calendar data travels only between Google and your computer. A copy of your messages may be kept in a local cache on your computer so the app is fast and works offline. Mailbox does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.

Sign-in credentials

Mailbox never sees your Google password. Access tokens are stored on your computer in Windows Credential Manager. You can revoke Mailbox's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by signing out in the app.

What Mailbox does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • It does not allow humans to read your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mailbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Mailbox and deleting its local data folder removes the local cache. Signing out removes the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/HecticMike/mailbox-site/issues.
